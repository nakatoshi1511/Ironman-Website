# News article thumbnail galleries

## Purpose

Show related photos for each article section as compact, browsable previews while retaining a single main image in the reading flow.

## Interaction

- A thumbnail strip appears directly below its associated main image.
- Selecting a thumbnail opens that image in the existing lightbox.
- The main image remains unchanged when a thumbnail is selected.

## Presentation

- The thumbnail strip uses the existing article image styling and lightbox.
- Desktop and mobile layouts keep the previews visually compact and free from horizontal page overflow.
- No captions, alt text, or other article content are added unless the user explicitly provides or approves them.
